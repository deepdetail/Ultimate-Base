Why the rules matter:
- **Magenta windows:** the game cuts the windows out and draws the real 3D world behind them.
- **No control in the cockpit:** a painted-in wheel can't turn. The game draws the separate control on top and moves it with your steering.
- **Controls centered on their pivot:** a wheel turns around its hub, and a stick tilts from its base. If the pivot is in a known spot, the motion looks right.
- **No brand names in the prompts:** real brand names are still to be replaced before the App Store, so the prompts describe each vehicle instead of naming it.

How to use it: open ChatGPT in Chrome, open the Claude side panel, copy everything below the line and paste it in. To continue another day, paste it again and say which number to start from. When the art lands in Drive, ask Claude to wire it in.
