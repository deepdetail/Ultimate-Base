# Claude in Chrome: the outside of the base, one painting per base Level (Levels 1 to 60)

What this makes in ChatGPT: 60 paintings of the front of the base, one for every base Level from 1 to 60, so the outside visibly grows at every Level. Levels 61 to 100 are the next batch.

How it works: each era starts with a fresh painting. Every Level after that edits the previous painting and adds ONE thing (a section, a wing, a component or extra fortification), so the building grows step by step and stays the same building. Each era stays in ONE ChatGPT chat, because ChatGPT can only edit pictures already in its chat.

| Era | Levels | Style |
|---|---|---|
| Minimalist Brutalist | 1 to 9 | concrete bunker that grows into a fortified compound |
| Standard Futuristic | 10 to 19 | sleek white and teal panels |
| Vintage Mid-Century | 20 to 34 | retro curves, cream and teal |
| Antique Grand | 35 to 49 | classical columns, cream stone, gold trim |
| Fortress Tech (restyled, was Ultra Modern) | 50 to 60 here, 69 later | armored, fortified high tech, not glossy luxury |

Why the rules matter:
- **Full roof in frame:** the game trims each painting to the building. If the roof runs off the top of the picture, the building gets cut off, which is what happened at Level 50.
- **Flat roof:** turrets stand only on flat, level surfaces, so the roof needs flat spots.
- **Plain background:** the magenta background lets the game cut the building out cleanly.

How to use it: open ChatGPT in Chrome, open the Claude side panel, copy everything below the line and paste it in. To continue another day, paste it again and say which Level to start from.

RULES (every painting):
- The FRONT of one building, straight-on at eye level, centered, landscape 3:2 (1536 x 1024).
- The WHOLE building in frame, including the complete roof and anything on it. Leave at least the top 12% of the picture empty above the highest point, and a margin at the left and right.
- The roof and the tops of wings and towers are FLAT and LEVEL, with at least four flat spots across the top wide enough for a turret to stand on. Spires and masts are fine, but keep flat roof around them.
- The building stands on a thin paved plinth along the bottom edge. Everything else is a flat pure magenta #FF00FF background: no sky, no landscape, no ground, no shadow on the background.
- No people, no vehicles, no animals, no text, no letters, no numbers, no signs with words, no logos, no symbols, no flags with designs, no borders, no watermarks.
- At the first Level of each era the building fills about half the width, so the additions fit. By the last Level of the era it fills about 85%.
