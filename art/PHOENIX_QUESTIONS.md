# Questions for Phoenix: the crew characters

Short answers are perfect. Skip anything you don't care about.

## Big picture
1. How many kinds of crew people should there be: 8, 12 or 16?
2. Should every kind come as both a boy and a girl version?
3. Should each kind have a special natural power (like stronger, better aim, smarter, faster)?

## Each character
For each one: what's their name or nickname, are they big, small, tall or skinny, what's one thing that makes them easy to spot (hair, glasses, freckles, a bandana), and what are they naturally best at?

Here's a starting list. Change anything:
