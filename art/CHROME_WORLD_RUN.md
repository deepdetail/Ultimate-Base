Why the rules matter:
- **Nothing in the scenery:** the game draws the base, the crew, the vehicles and the raiders on top of these pictures, so they stay empty landscapes.
- **Open middles and flat bottoms:** the base stands in the middle of the backdrop, and the raid backdrop needs flat ground across the bottom for the enemy base and fighters.
- **Sides that look alike:** the mission skies repeat all the way around the horizon (the game mirrors them), so the left and right edges should look similar.
- **Underground pictures repeat downward:** the game puts the top picture right under the ground and repeats the deep one below it, mirroring every other copy to hide the seams.

How to use it: open ChatGPT in Chrome, open the Claude side panel, copy everything below the line and paste it in. To continue another day, paste it again and say which number to start from. When the art lands in Drive, ask Claude to wire it in.
